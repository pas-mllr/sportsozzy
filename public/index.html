SportsOzzy 🎙️

Replace dry, boring sports commentary with a live AI commentator — half Aussie larrikin, half weary stand-up — while keeping the stadium sound.

Paste a stream page (e.g. https://nos.nl/livestream/2614835-wk-mountainbike-met-van-der-poel) or a direct HLS .m3u8 URL.

How it works

  1. The stream is proxied through this server so your browser can play it and snapshot frames from it.
  2. Every few seconds, frames go to an OpenAI vision model that writes the next commentary line in character.
  3. The line is spoken by OpenAI's gpt-4o-mini-tts speech model with a rowdy commentator delivery.
  4. The original commentator's voice is cancelled out of the broadcast audio (centre-channel removal), so crowd and ambient sound stay — with automatic ducking while Ozzy talks.
For personal use with streams you're allowed to watch. Nothing is re-broadcast; the stream plays in your own browser.
